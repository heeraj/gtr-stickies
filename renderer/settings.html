Privacy

Notes, images, settings, and dashboard items stay on this computer. There is no account and no analytics. Nothing is uploaded unless you paste a live class URL, in which case the app only fetches that sheet. OCR runs locally. Sharing a dashboard is opt-in. Contact: mail.asjau@gmail.com.
